# SOP 02: Write LinkedIn Post (Hook + Body + CTA)

## Purpose
Take a post idea and turn it into a full, ready-to-publish LinkedIn post in Nick's voice.

## How to Use in Cowork
Ask Claude: "Write a LinkedIn post about [topic or idea from SOP 01]"

## Prompt

```
You are a world class copywriter writing a LinkedIn post for Nick Sarafa, founder of Light School.

Read these files before writing:
- 2-context/me.md (Nick's story and beliefs)
- 2-context/brand-guidelines.md (tone of voice and copywriting rules)
- 1-strategy/strategy.md (content strategy)

TOPIC: [INSERT TOPIC OR IDEA HERE]

Write a LinkedIn post following these rules:

STRUCTURE:
1. HOOK (first 1-2 lines): Stop the scroll. Make them click "see more." This is the most important part.
2. BODY (5-15 lines): Deliver the story, insight, or value. One sentence per line. Mix punchy one-liners with deeper riffs.
3. CTA (last 1-2 lines): Clear next step. Either "Join my free workshop [link]" or "DM me [specific word]" or a question to drive comments.

VOICE RULES:
- Conversational, direct, irreverent
- Short punchy bursts mixed with raw depth
- Self-aware humor
- Clarity over polish (voice note energy)
- Personal and real (reference real experiences)
- One sentence per line
- NEVER use em-dashes
- Parentheses for insider asides (like this)
- Specific numbers always
- Call out the reader mid-thought
- Casual profanity sparingly
- CAPITALIZE for emphasis when it makes sense
- Make it sound like a personal voice note

NEVER:
- Use em-dashes (--)
- Sound like a thought leader or guru
- Be generic or vague
- Write walls of text
- Use corporate jargon
- End without a CTA

Output the post ready to copy-paste into LinkedIn. Nothing else.
```

## Expected Output
A complete LinkedIn post ready to publish. Copy-paste format, no extra commentary.
