# LinkedIn Content Strategy

## Goal for Posts
Every post should strengthen one of these outcomes:
1. **Build institutional credibility** for c-ECO and the Johann Christian Hasse Foundation.
2. **Educate serious readers** on systemic risk, Earth system integrity, legal temporality, and predictive governance.
3. **Attract aligned collaborators** for research, governance, advisory, fellowship, funding, and Living Lab work.
4. **Document the build in public** without exposing confidential materials, private negotiations, or premature claims.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not post generic climate anxiety, AI hype, legal theory, or nonprofit language without a concrete c-ECO angle.
- Do not exaggerate institutional status, funding, partnerships, scientific certainty, or implementation readiness.
- Do not imply regulatory approval, official endorsement, or deployed authority unless it is formally true.
- Do not sound like a corporate press release.
- Do not post dense walls of text. Preserve intellectual weight, but make the post readable.
- Do not attack people or institutions. Critique systems, incentives, design failures, and governance gaps.
- Do not use casual guru language, viral bait, or "DM me [keyword]" CTAs.
- Do not disclose confidential foundation, candidate, grant, donor, legal, or governance materials.

## Types of Content
1. **Founder / Architect Reflection** - Why this work exists, what is being built, what problem it answers.
2. **Governance Insight** - Board design, committee architecture, fiduciary duties, institutional safeguards.
3. **Scientific Governance Explainer** - Systemic risk, thresholds, resilience, irreversibility, early warning.
4. **Legal Architecture** - Temporal validity of law, ex ante governance, reversibility before harm, contract design.
5. **Build in Public** - New pages, frameworks, assessment tools, fellowship materials, living lab architecture.
6. **Funding and Institution Building** - Grant readiness, restricted funding risk, long-term funding architecture.
7. **Partnership Invitation** - Clear invitations to serious researchers, funders, advisors, legal scholars, and institutions.

## Content Pillars
1. c-ECO doctrine and predictive governance.
2. Johann Christian Hasse Foundation governance and institutional design.
3. Earth system risk, resilience, thresholds, and complex systems.
4. Law, fiduciary responsibility, contracts, and temporal integrity.
5. TFP, TDR, Living Labs, fellowship, and applied implementation pathways.
6. Founder perspective: building a serious institution before the world fully knows how to name the problem.

## Posting Cadence
- **Frequency**: 3-5 posts per week is preferred over low-quality daily posting.
- **Best times**: Weekday mornings in the target audience's main timezone.
- **Consistency**: Intellectual continuity matters more than volume.
- **Rhythm**: Alternate between conceptual posts, practical governance posts, and public build updates.

## Post Structure
Every post should follow this skeleton:
1. **Hook**: A precise tension, claim, or question that serious people recognize.
2. **Context**: Explain the institutional, scientific, legal, or governance problem.
3. **c-ECO Angle**: Show how the Foundation, c-ECO, TFP, TDR, or Living Labs responds.
4. **Takeaway**: Give the reader one clean concept to remember.
5. **CTA**: Invite serious conversation, review, collaboration, or reading a specific page.

## Preferred CTAs
- "If this is the kind of governance problem you work on, I would be glad to connect."
- "The Foundation is building this carefully. Serious feedback is welcome."
- "For researchers, funders, and governance practitioners working in this space, this is an open invitation to talk."
- "I will share more as the architecture develops."
- "Read more: [insert relevant c-eco.org link]"
