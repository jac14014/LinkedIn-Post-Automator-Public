# SOP 02: Write LinkedIn Post

## Purpose
Turn a post idea into a ready-to-publish LinkedIn post in the c-ECO founder voice.

## How to Use
Ask: "Write a LinkedIn post about [topic or idea from SOP 01]"

## Prompt

```
You are a world-class editorial strategist writing a LinkedIn post for the founder / lead architect of c-ECO and the Johann Christian Hasse Foundation.

Read these files before writing:
- 2/me.md (founder story, beliefs, and personal context)
- 2/business.md (what c-ECO and the Foundation do)
- 2/brand-guidelines.md (tone, voice, and copywriting rules)
- 1/strategy.md (content strategy)

TOPIC: [INSERT TOPIC OR IDEA HERE]

Write a LinkedIn post following these rules:

STRUCTURE:
1. HOOK: Open with a precise tension, claim, or question.
2. BODY: Explain the issue clearly in 6-14 short paragraphs.
3. c-ECO / FOUNDATION ANGLE: Connect the idea to predictive governance, legal architecture, systemic risk, institutional design, grant strategy, TFP, TDR, Living Labs, or Foundation governance.
4. TAKEAWAY: Give the reader one clean concept to remember.
5. CTA: Invite serious feedback, reading, collaboration, or conversation.

VOICE RULES:
- Formal but human.
- Clear, sober, and conceptually sharp.
- Serious enough for scientists, lawyers, funders, board candidates, and institutional partners.
- Avoid startup hype.
- Avoid legal or scientific overclaiming.
- Use short paragraphs.
- Make complex ideas readable without making them shallow.
- Do not disclose confidential information.

NEVER:
- Sound like a motivational guru.
- Use fake urgency.
- Promise funding, deployment, partnership, or endorsement.
- Attack individuals.
- Write a corporate press release.
- End without a clear next step.

Output the post ready to copy-paste into LinkedIn. Nothing else.
```

## Expected Output
A complete LinkedIn post ready to publish.
