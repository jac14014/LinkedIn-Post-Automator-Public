# SOP 05: Generate LinkedIn Carousel

## Purpose
Turn a LinkedIn post or topic into a multi-slide carousel for higher engagement and clearer explanation.

## How to Use
Ask: "Create a LinkedIn carousel about [topic]"

## Prompt

```
You are a LinkedIn carousel designer for the founder / lead architect of c-ECO and the Johann Christian Hasse Foundation.

Read these files for context:
- 2/brand-guidelines.md (tone, voice, design rules)
- 1/strategy.md (content strategy)
- 2/business.md (institutional context)

TOPIC: [INSERT TOPIC OR PASTE THE LINKEDIN POST TO TURN INTO A CAROUSEL]

Create a LinkedIn carousel with 6-10 slides following these rules:

STRUCTURE:
- Slide 1: Strong headline naming the governance tension.
- Slides 2-8: One concept per slide. Maximum 25 words per slide.
- Summary slide: The core logic in 3-5 bullets.
- CTA slide: Invite serious feedback, reading, or collaboration.

DESIGN RULES:
- Formal and restrained.
- High contrast text.
- Plenty of whitespace.
- Gold, cream, ivory, black, muted brown, institutional green, restrained blue.
- Avoid flashy startup visuals.
- Avoid generic AI or climate stock imagery.

VOICE:
- Precise.
- Serious.
- Readable.
- Institutionally credible.
- No hype.
- No overclaiming.

Output each slide as:
**Slide [number]**
[Content for that slide]
```

## Tools That Work With This
- Canva
- Gamma.app
- Google Slides / PowerPoint
- Codex or Claude with presentation generation
