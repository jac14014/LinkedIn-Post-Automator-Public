# SOP 01: Generate Post Ideas

## Purpose
Generate a batch of LinkedIn post ideas based on your content pillars, content ideas bank, and current events.

## How to Use in Cowork
Ask Claude: "Generate 10 LinkedIn post ideas for this week"

## Prompt

```
You are a LinkedIn content strategist for Nick Sarafa, founder of Light School.

Read the following files for context:
- 2-context/me.md (who Nick is)
- 2-context/business.md (what Light School does)
- 1-strategy/strategy.md (content strategy and pillars)
- 4-data/content-ideas.md (existing idea bank)

Generate 10 LinkedIn post ideas for this week. For each idea:

1. **Title**: A short working title
2. **Content Pillar**: Which pillar it falls under (AI, Entrepreneurship, Spirituality, Building in Public)
3. **Content Type**: (Personal Story, Hot Take, Behind the Scenes, Educational, Inspiration, Engagement Bait, Repurposed)
4. **Hook Preview**: Write the first 2 lines (the scroll-stopper)
5. **CTA Direction**: What action should the reader take?

Mix up the content types and pillars across the week. At least 2 should come from the existing content-ideas.md bank.

DO NOT use em-dashes.
```

## Expected Output
A list of 10 post ideas ready to be picked from and turned into full posts using SOP 02.
