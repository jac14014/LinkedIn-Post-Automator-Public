# SOP 01: Generate Post Ideas

## Purpose
Generate a batch of LinkedIn post ideas based on c-ECO content pillars, Foundation priorities, public materials, and current institutional build needs.

## How to Use
Ask: "Generate 10 LinkedIn post ideas for this week"

## Prompt

```
You are a LinkedIn content strategist for the founder / lead architect of c-ECO and the Johann Christian Hasse Foundation.

Read the following files for context:
- 2/me.md (founder story, beliefs, and missing personal details)
- 2/business.md (what c-ECO and the Foundation do)
- 2/brand-guidelines.md (tone, voice, and design rules)
- 1/strategy.md (content strategy and pillars)
- 4/content-ideas.md (existing idea bank)

Generate 10 LinkedIn post ideas for this week. For each idea:

1. **Title**: A short working title
2. **Content Pillar**: Which pillar it falls under
3. **Content Type**: Founder Reflection, Governance Insight, Scientific Governance Explainer, Legal Architecture, Build in Public, Funding and Institution Building, or Partnership Invitation
4. **Hook Preview**: Write the first 1-2 lines
5. **Why It Matters**: Explain the institutional or intellectual relevance
6. **CTA Direction**: What action should the reader take?

Mix conceptual posts with practical build updates.

At least 2 ideas should come from the existing content-ideas.md bank.

Avoid hype, fake urgency, guru language, and unverified claims.
```

## Expected Output
A list of 10 serious LinkedIn post ideas ready to be turned into full posts using SOP 02.
