## Why These Work

✓ **Behind-the-scenes authenticity** - Real dev workspace, not staged corporate  
✓ **Location specificity** - Lisbon reference anchors Nick's actual context  
✓ **Technical credibility** - Visible code/interface without being generic  
✓ **Cozy startup energy** - Coffee, plants, sunlight vs. sterile office  
✓ **LinkedIn-optimized** - Both formats work well; 16:9 catches feeds, 1:1 is classic  
✓ **No text** - Image stands alone, caption tells the story  
✓ **Narrative potential** - Tells story of "built in 45 minutes" at a glance  

---

## Tips for Generation

- Use **Midjourney v6** or **DALL-E 3** for best results (photorealism)
- **Ideogram** works well for modern tech lifestyle aesthetic
- Add detail modifiers: "shallow depth of field", "Fujifilm color grading", "cinematic lighting"
- Avoid generic stock photo terms (delete: "stock photo", "professional", "corporate", "business")
- If regenerating: emphasize "authentic developer workspace" over "office" or "desk"
