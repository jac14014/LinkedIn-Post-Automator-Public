# Draft - Signals Without Governance

A risk signal without governance is just anxiety with data.

That is the problem with many early warning systems.

They can detect.

They can classify.

They can alert.

But they do not always answer the institutional question:

Who must do what, by when, under which duty, with what record?

That is why c-ECO treats signal processing and governance architecture as one problem.

The value is not only in seeing risk earlier.

The value is in making earlier action legitimate, accountable, and institutionally possible.
