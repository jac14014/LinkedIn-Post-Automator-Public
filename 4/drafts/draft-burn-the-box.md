They used to say "think outside the box."

Then it became "there is no box."

Now? The box is on fire and AI is holding the match.

Here's what I mean.

The "box" was always just constraints. Money. Time. Technical skill. Access.

You needed $50,000 to build an app.
You needed a degree to understand the code.
You needed 6 months to test the idea.
You needed a team to ship it.

Every single one of those walls just evaporated.

I watched a founder in my program go from a napkin sketch to a working prototype in 4 days. No code. No CTO. No fundraising deck.

Just clarity and a conversation with Claude.

The people still "thinking outside the box" are playing an old game.

They're optimizing around constraints that don't exist anymore.

Meanwhile the ones who get it are asking a completely different question.

Not "how do I get around this wall?"

But "wait... where did the wall go?"

That's the shift. That's the moment.

The box didn't just open. It burned down.

And the founders who realize it first are going to build things that make the last decade look like a warmup.
