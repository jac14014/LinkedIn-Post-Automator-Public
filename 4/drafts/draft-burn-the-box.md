# Draft - The Box Is the Wrong Unit

Many institutions still treat systemic risk as if it arrives inside neat categories.

Environmental.

Financial.

Legal.

Operational.

Political.

But systemic risk does not respect the boxes we use to manage it.

It moves through them.

That is why governance architecture matters.

If the risk crosses domains, the decision system must also cross domains.

c-ECO is built around that premise.

Not because categories are useless.

Because categories become dangerous when they hide transmission.
