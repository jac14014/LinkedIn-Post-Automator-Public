# LinkedIn Content Strategy

## Goal for Posts
Every post should drive one of these actions:
1. **Join a free workshop** - Get people into the Light School ecosystem
2. **Send me a DM** - Start a conversation that leads to enrollment

## What NOT to Do
- Don't post generic AI hype with no personal take
- Don't use thought-leader jargon or guru speak
- Don't post without a clear CTA
- Don't use em-dashes (--)
- Don't use "DM me [keyword]" CTAs (e.g. "DM me 'build'"). It's gimmicky. Use natural conversation starters instead.
- Don't post polished corporate content (keep it raw and real)
- Don't post walls of text with no line breaks
- Don't be preachy. Be fun and educational (funducational)

## Types of Content
1. **Personal Story** - Your journey, failures, pivots, lessons
2. **Hot Take / Contrarian** - Punch holes in common advice, show receipts
3. **Behind the Scenes** - What you're building, client wins, real numbers
4. **Educational / Tutorial** - AI tips, tools, frameworks (beginner-friendly)
5. **Inspiration / Motivation** - Short punchy wisdom drops
6. **Engagement Bait** - Questions, polls, "what would you do?" scenarios
7. **Repurposed Content** - From blog posts, workshops, Apple Notes ideas

## Content Pillars
1. AI + Technology (making it accessible)
2. Entrepreneurship (non-technical founders)
3. Spirituality + Purpose (mission-driven work)
4. Building in Public (transparency, real numbers)

## Posting Cadence
- **Frequency**: One post per day
- **Best times**: Weekday mornings (8-10am target audience timezone)
- **Consistency**: Daily posting beats viral posting

## Post Structure
Every post follows this skeleton:
1. **Hook** (first 1-2 lines) - Stop the scroll. Make them click "see more"
2. **Body** (3-10 lines) - Deliver the value, story, or insight
3. **CTA** (last 1-2 lines) - Tell them what to do next
